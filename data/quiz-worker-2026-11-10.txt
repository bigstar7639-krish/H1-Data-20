Question: Scotch whisky and Drambuie make up which cocktail?

Answer: Rusty Nail - General Knowledge. Itha kelvi Sea pathi - Answer Rusty Nail. Sea pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Sea pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Sea oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Sea ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Sea la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
